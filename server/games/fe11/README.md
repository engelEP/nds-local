# Fire Emblem: Shadow Dragon

The server covers the online shop and the loan of units.

The shop is the armory past the counter. The game builds the stock from this server's date, so changing the clock on the console does not change what is for sale. It can be opened from chapter 2 onward.

Lending units uses the two options in the game, Send and Download. Send leaves units on a profile. Download takes units that profile is offering.

The test units are on the profile you add with friend code `103979215105`. Download from that friend to borrow them.

## Shop calendar

The stock follows the server's date and changes at 9:00 in the morning, UTC.

Every month has the same pattern.

- From the 1st at 9:00 until the 11th at 9:00: Killer Lance, Armorslayer, and Poleax.
- From the 11th at 9:00 until the 21st at 9:00: Killer Axe, Ridersbane, and Wyrmslayer.
- From the 21st at 9:00 until the 1st of the next month at 9:00: Killing Edge, Levin Sword, Dragonpike, and Hammer.
- A day whose date ends in 1, from 9:00 until the next day at 9:00: Elysian Whip. That is the 1st, 11th, 21st, and 31st.
- A day whose date ends in 3: Wo Dao. That is the 3rd, 13th, and 23rd.
- A day whose date ends in 5: Master Seal. That is the 5th, 15th, and 25th.
- A day whose date ends in 7: Longbow. That is the 7th, 17th, and 27th.

The silver weapons rotate by the Mondays of the month, each from 9:00 to the next Monday at 9:00.

- Silver Sword: the first Monday until the second Monday.
- Silver Lance: the second Monday until the third Monday.
- Silver Axe: the third Monday until the fourth Monday.
- Silver Bow: from the fourth Monday until the first Monday of the next month. It is also in the shop from the start of the month until that first Monday.

The brave weapons are in the shop from Saturday at 9:00 until the following Monday at 9:00.

- Brave Sword: the first Saturday of the month.
- Brave Lance: the second Saturday.
- Brave Axe: the third Saturday.
- Brave Bow: the fourth Saturday.

Steel Sword, Steel Lance, Steel Axe, Steel Bow, Javelin, Hand Axe, Thunder, Blizzard, Mend, Master Key, Vulnerary, and Pure Water stay in the shop all month.
